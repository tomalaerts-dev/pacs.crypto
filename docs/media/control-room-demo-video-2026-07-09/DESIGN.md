# Design identity — pacs.crypto Control Room video

Style prompt: professional regulated-finance product demo, dark control-room UI, calm technical launch video.

Colors:
- Canvas: #06080D
- Panel: #0A0E16
- Raised: #0D1220
- Border: #1C2434
- Highlight blue: #4D9DF6
- Success green: #3DD68C
- Body text: #C6D2E4
- Muted: #8B96AB

Typography:
- IBM Plex Sans for narration overlays and headings.
- IBM Plex Mono for labels, identifiers, and route names.

Motion:
- Smooth, calm, precise. No hype flashes.
- App window is the hero; overlays are restrained.
- Use section labels and a progress line rather than cluttered callouts.

What not to do:
- Do not imply production readiness, certification, standardization, or real funded Sepolia execution.
- Do not use fake secrets, credential prompts, or private path strings.
- Do not bury the product UI behind generic slides.
